# 16. Open questions

These are the decisions that the spec does not make. Each one has the options, a recommendation, the milestone by which it must be decided, and who decides.
- "Owner" means the owner of the repositories.
- "Maintainers" means the Mathlib maintainers, asked on the Lean Zulip or by letter.
- "Reviewers" means the outside reviewers of 14.5.

A decision goes into the CHANGELOG of `tamnd/mathlib` with its date, and the row here gets the word **decided** and a link. A recommendation is not a decision until the owner signs it.

## 16.1 Questions for the Mathlib maintainers

These go in the post on Zulip and the letter of M0.

1. Is the name `tamnd/mathlib` a problem (Q-01)? Would you prefer another name for a project that is not a fork?
2. What form of attribution do you want for quoted signatures and docstrings (Q-09)?
3. May the companion register in `downstream-reports` (Q-11), given that it has examples and exercises and no new mathematics?
4. Which kinds of small pull requests from the project are welcome (Q-15): missing simple lemmas, docstring fixes, naming fixes?
5. Would you like the reviewed E1 sentences as docstrings, for declarations that have none (Q-16)?
6. Is there a plan for a textbook of the mathematics of Mathlib that this project should join instead (Q-17)?
7. Which areas have a design that a textbook reader often gets wrong, so that the Mathlib boxes of those areas need the most care? The project's first list is in 03.2 and the junk value table of 03.4.

## 16.2 Questions for the area reviewers

1. Is the order of the chapters of your volume (06.2, `blueprint/sections/<vol>.yaml`) one in which you would teach the area?
2. Is the E3 list of your volume (06.3) right? What is missing that a graduate student must know, and is in Mathlib?
3. Which textbook should be the spine of your volume (06.2)?
4. Which results of your area does Mathlib state in a form that differs from the textbooks, so that the Mathlib box must explain it?
5. Would you read the sections of one chapter as a `full` review (14.5), or the mathematics or the Mathlib boxes only?
