# Outreach drafts

This directory has the drafts of the post on the Lean Zulip and the letters of M0 (spec 15, 16.1 and 16.2). They are drafts. Nobody sent them. The owner reads each draft, changes it, signs it and sends it. A model must not send them.

| File | To | Where | Questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| [zulip-post.md](zulip-post.md) | the Lean community | the Lean Zulip, channel `#mathlib4`, topic "The Mathlib Handbook" | 16.1, in short form |
| [letter-maintainers.md](letter-maintainers.md) | the Mathlib maintainers | a direct message on Zulip or a letter, after the post | 16.1 (Q-01, Q-09, Q-11, Q-15, Q-16, Q-17) |
| [letter-reviewers.md](letter-reviewers.md) | an expert of one area | a letter, one for each volume | 16.2 |

Before you send a draft, do these steps:

1. Replace each text in square brackets, for example `[the name of the owner]`.
2. Check the numbers against the site on the day you send. The numbers in the drafts are from 2026-11-26.
3. After you send it, add the date and the link to the table of `outreach/sent.md`, and tick the item of the M0 checklist in tamnd/mathlib#1.
4. Write each answer to an open question in the CHANGELOG with its date (spec 16).
