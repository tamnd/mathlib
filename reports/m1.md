## The depth of the declarations

The six volumes of the Catalogue have 92,590 declarations. This table compares the computed depth of 04.4 with the estimate of 04.7.

| Depth | Estimate (04.7) | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| E3 | 4% to 6% | 1.6% |
| E2 | 10% to 15% | 0.4% |
| E1 | about 40% | 54.6% |
| E0 | 35% to 45% | 43.4% |

The share of E0 agrees with the estimate. The shares of E3 and E2 are much lower.

The low share of E2 has a cause in the index. Rule 2 of 04.4 makes a theorem E2 when five declarations of other modules use it. `scripts/Index.lean` of mathlib-lean records the constants of the type of each declaration and the constants of the value of a definition. It does not record the constants of the proof of a theorem. A theorem is almost never in the type of another declaration, so its used-by count is almost always 0. The fix is to record the theorems that each proof uses, as a separate field of the index, and to count them in `mlib depth`. This needs a new index on a build server. The work is in the checklist of milestone M2 (#3).

The low share of E3 has two causes. Many module docstrings have no heading "Main results", and the rule of the top 2% applies only to classes and structures. The E3 share is measured again after the fix of the index.
